# Enspiral Forge

### Ship stuff that matters

Enspiral Forge is an [Enspiral Node](https://handbook.enspiral.com/agreements/nodes) focused on **doing and shipping**. Called into being at the Enspiral Open Space near Wellington on 31 January 2026.

---

## What is the Forge?

Do you have a project that you're sitting on that you'd love to launch into the world? Something that could benefit from the fresh perspectives of smart, experienced and generous people? Do you want some rigour and accountability?

The Forge runs in **10-week seasons**. Each season is an incubator to get your project off the ground — with peer coaching, accountability, and a Demo Day to share your work with the network.

## What to expect

- **Weekly calls** — the core rhythm of each season
- **Peer coaching** — wise crowds, troika consulting, and complexity-informed patterns
- **Rigour and accountability** — this is a doing space
- **Demo Day** — ship your project in Week 10 and share it with the network
- **Giga-brains and mega-hearts** — tap into the collective intelligence of Enspiral

## How to join

Seasons are invite-only. Each node member can invite up to three people. We also reserve spots for people outside the Enspiral network.

**Season fee:** $200–$300 NZD sliding scale. Full scholarships available.
