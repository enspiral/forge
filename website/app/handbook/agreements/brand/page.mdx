# Brand Agreement

As an Enspiral Node, we operate under the Enspiral brand and are bound by the network's [Brand Agreement](https://handbook.enspiral.com/agreements/brand).

## Brand ownership

All ownership and control over Enspiral-branded domain names, trademarks, and social media accounts is held by the Enspiral Foundation. This includes any accounts or domains created for Forge node activities that use the Enspiral name.

## Commercial use

Any commercial use of the Enspiral brand beyond running Enspiral gatherings requires approval from the Foundation Board or the Brand Working Group.

Standard node activities — running seasons, hosting gatherings, and internal communications — do not require separate brand approval.
