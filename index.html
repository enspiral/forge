What it is

Ship It runs in seasons —

intensive 8-week cohorts built around peer coaching, weekly commitments, and a real deadline. By Week 8, you ship. Not "plan to ship." Not "almost ready." Ship.

We use liberating structures, design thinking, somatic exercises, and honest conversation. You bring the project. We bring each other.

01

Weekly calls

Show up, share where you are, get unstuck. Weeks 1, 5, and 8 run 90 minutes. The rest are one hour.

02

Peer coaching

Small groups. Wise crowds. Troika consulting. Feedback from people who've built things and learned things the hard way.

03

Your commitment

Each week you tell your peers what you'll do. Then you do it and you log it. Ship It is a level up in accountability.

08

Demo Day

Week 8. You present your project to the whole Enspiral network. There is no better forcing function than an audience.

What to expect

Giga-brains and mega-hearts, applied to your work.

Peer coaching

Structured feedback from people who've shipped things and know the feeling of being stuck.

Social Lean Canvas

Rapid-clarity tools to stress-test your idea without drowning in planning.

Liberating structures

Wise crowds, Troika consulting, and other complexity-informed patterns for when the map runs out.

Somatic exercises

We ship better when we're in our bodies. Brief practices to settle the nervous system before we work.

Your big questions

Space to surface what's actually blocking you — not just the tactical stuff.

Human accountability

You'll tell people what you're going to do. They'll ask you about it next week. It works.

About

Built on a decade of doing.

Enspiral Forge is a Node of the Enspiral network — a community of people who've been building companies, collectives, and experiments in a better way of working since 2010. The Forge is the part of that network focused on action: bring a project, get it out the door.

The Forge was called into being at an Open Space near Wellington in January 2026. Ship It is its first programme.
